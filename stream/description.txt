An AI is trying to beat Factorio, all the way to launching a rocket, entirely on its own.

No human touches the game. The AI can't even see the screen: it reads the map as text, plans the factory, builds it through a game API, and repairs its own mistakes. Everything you see on screen was placed by the bot.

When it gets truly stuck, the stream switches to "Self-upgrading". The problem is analysed, the bot's code is improved, and then it either continues from where it stopped or starts a fresh run, whichever keeps the progress steady. The counters at the top show which run this is and how many times it has upgraded itself.

WHAT'S ON SCREEN
Top: progress toward the rocket, splits against its best earlier run, run number and self-upgrades
Right: what the AI is thinking (strategist, overseer, assessor)
Bottom: the current instruction and the last goals it finished
